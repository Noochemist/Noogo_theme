# A Study in Scarlet — opening (Arthur Conan Doyle)

Excerpt from *A Study in Scarlet* (1887), used to demonstrate block dialog, images, and code blocks.

## Prologue

> In the year 1878 I took my degree of Doctor of Medicine of the University of London, and proceeded to Netley to go through the course prescribed for surgeons in the army.

So begins Watson's account, before he ever met Sherlock Holmes.

## About the detective

At first I took him for **no more** than a researcher in chemistry; only later did I learn how far his genius in the art of detection exceeded a common man's reach:

- He commanded chemistry, anatomy, and a vast index of crime archives;
- He could infer, from a single strand of hair, how far the man had travelled;
- Over and again he bade me to *see* rather than merely *look*.

A short exchange (dialog block):

> "You see, but you do not observe. The distinction is clear."
